Crafting Fragrances. Building Brands. Since 1995.

Established in 1995, we bring three decades of expertise in the fragrance industry, combining the art of perfumery with modern manufacturing capabilities. Today, our business extends across 5+ countries, including the USA, UK, Australia, and Tanzania, serving brands and businesses across international markets.

Our expertise spans fine fragrance compounds, home & personal care fragrances, incense fragrances, diffuser oils, and air care solutions. Beyond fragrance creation, we specialise in end-to-end contract manufacturing, helping brands develop and manufacture spray perfumes, deodorants, aerosol products, car fresheners, room fresheners, and other fragrance-led products.

From concept and fragrance development to manufacturing, filling, packaging, and finished products, we provide an integrated solution under one roof — helping both emerging and established brands bring their ideas to market with consistency, quality, and reliability.

From fragrance to finished product, we help bring brands to life.
